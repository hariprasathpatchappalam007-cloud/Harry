How the implemented fee is calculated

A one-page summary of PROC_Lead_Arrangement_FEE and its current seeded promotion rules. This describes the implementation, not a proposed fee policy.

Which master supplies which rate?

Arrangement Fee Master (LEAD_ARR_FEE_MASTER)

This is the source of the base fee rates. The procedure matches a row by product, subtype, residency and staff status, then applies its component percentages to the corresponding finance and other amounts. The row also supplies the minimum, maximum, APP/ACC split and VAT setting.

Promotion Master (Lead_Promotion_master)

This supplies a promotion discount, not the component fee rates. Its discount percentage may reduce the calculated fee later, when the promotion configuration allows it and an eligible Active row passes the procedure's date check. Special promotion rules are stored separately.

ACC is the ACC allocation portion of the total arrangement fee, returned as ARRANGEMENT_FEE_ACC. It is not a separate rate. The procedure does not document a formal full expansion of the acronym.

Use the Lead Fee Master Maker to access the Arrangement Fee Master and Promotion Master.

Calculation sequence

01 | MATCHFind the fee rowProduct, subtype, residency and staff
02 | ADJUST RATESApply pre-calculation rulesPromotion-specific component rates
03 | CALCULATEBuild the base feeComponent amounts, then min/max
04 | APPLY EXCEPTIONSRun later rulesProject, promotion and master discount
05 | FINALIZEVAT and outputReturn total and APP/ACC amounts
Base fee = sum of each fee-master rate (%) multiplied by its corresponding finance, property, buyout, top-up, equity, cash-out or MBRE amount.

A missing matching fee-master row stops calculation with an arrangement-fee-not-defined error. This guide omits the STL/NSTL override branch.

Base fee and limits

Non-staff customers use the component calculation above. The procedure applies the matched row's minimum and maximum unless a configured promotion skips a limit.

Staff customers take a flat base fee of 3,500, allocated as APP 0 and ACC 3,500, instead of the standard percentage-and-cap calculation.

SituationImplemented maximum adjustment
Standard Ijara / Standard60,000 when finance is above 7 million and below 15 million; 100,000 at 15 million or more.
Resident / Company Resident, qualifying Commercial, Equity Release or Standard Ijara Buyout/Topup cases60,000 through 15 million; 250,000 above 15 million.
Other productsUses the matched fee-row maximum, with the general 15-million adjustment where applicable. Subvention subtypes are excluded from that general adjustment; a separate resident/non-staff subvention rule applies.

Project exception

For project ID PRE_APPROVAL, after post-base promotion rules:

If ACC is non-zero, the total becomes 1,500, APP becomes 0, and ACC becomes 1,500. If ACC is zero, all fee amounts become zero. Staff status is reset to No.

Discount and VAT order

Eligible master discount is applied after the pre-master-discount rules. Post-master rules then run, followed by VAT when applicable. VAT percentage comes from the shared lead constant.

The current master-discount date condition checks start date on/after today and end date on/before today; confirm this coded behavior before relying on dated discounts.
